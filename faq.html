Rooms & Amenities

Getting Here & Addis Ababa

Travel tips

Approximately 15 minutes and 8 kilometers, depending on traffic. See our Location & Directions page for full transport details.

Most nationalities can obtain an e-Visa or visa-on-arrival for Ethiopia, but requirements change — always confirm current visa policy with an official government source before traveling.

The local currency is the Ethiopian Birr (ETB). The hotel accepts major international cards; US dollars are widely accepted for some services but local currency is recommended for everyday spending.

The Kazanchis Business District is well-trafficked and generally considered safe during daylight hours. As in any city, our Concierge is happy to advise on specific areas and times.
