  2. Privacy Policy

1. Who this policy covers

This Privacy Policy explains how The Amba Addis ("we," "us," "our") collects, uses, and protects personal information from visitors to this website (theambaaddis.rann.workers.dev) and from guests who make enquiries or reservation requests through it. It does not cover information collected in person during a stay, which is addressed separately in our in-hotel guest registration process.

2. Information we collect

Through this website, we may collect the following categories of information when you voluntarily provide it:

  • Contact details — name, email address, phone number, country (contact form, booking form, newsletter signup)
  • Stay details — check-in/check-out dates, room preference, number of guests, special requests (booking form)
  • Message content — anything you write in the contact form's message field
  • Technical data — browser type, device type, and general usage patterns via standard web server logs (not third-party advertising trackers)

3. How we use your information

Information collected through this site is used to: respond to reservation requests and general enquiries; confirm and manage bookings; send requested newsletter communications (only if you opt in); and improve this website's content and performance. We do not sell personal information to third parties.

4. Sharing your information

We may share information with service providers who help us operate the hotel and this website — for example, our payment processor Chapa (and, once available, Telebirr) to process a reservation payment, an email service provider for newsletter delivery, or a website hosting provider. These providers are only given the information necessary to perform their function and are expected to protect it accordingly.

5. Data retention

We retain reservation-related personal information for as long as necessary to fulfill the purpose it was collected for and to meet legal, accounting, or tax record-keeping requirements. Newsletter contact information is retained until you unsubscribe.

6. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information, and to withdraw consent for communications such as our newsletter at any time. To make a request, contact us using the details below.

7. Cookies

This website itself does not set tracking or advertising cookies. If analytics, live chat, or a booking engine is added in production, this section should be updated to disclose exactly what's used and to provide a way to manage cookie preferences where required by law.

8. Children's privacy

This website is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children through it.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision.
