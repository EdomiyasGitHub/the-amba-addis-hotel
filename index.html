Welcome

Rooted in place, built for how you actually travel.

The Amba Addis takes its name from the flat-topped highland plateaus that shape Ethiopia's landscape — a fitting namesake for a hotel built on quiet elevation above the everyday.

Set in the Kazanchis business district, minutes from Bole International Airport and the city's diplomatic quarter, we designed this hotel for guests who notice details: hand-finished furniture from regional workshops, a restaurant that treats Ethiopian coffee culture as the centerpiece it deserves to be, and service that remembers your name by the second morning.
