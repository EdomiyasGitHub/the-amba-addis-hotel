Getting Here

From the airport, and around the city

Transport options and estimated travel time to The Amba Addis
From By Approx. Time Approx. Distance
Bole International Airport (ADD) Hotel shuttle or taxi 15 minutes 8 km
Meskel Square On foot 5 minutes 0.4 km
National Museum of Ethiopia Taxi or ride-hailing app 10 minutes 4 km
UN Economic Commission for Africa On foot 6 minutes 0.5 km
Addis Ababa Bole Light Rail Station On foot 8 minutes 0.6 km

Airport shuttle service is complimentary on request — arrange through the Front Desk or when confirming your reservation. Ride-hailing apps operate throughout Addis Ababa.

Nearby

Worth the short walk

  • Meskel Square — the city's civic heart, 5 minutes on foot.

  • National Museum of Ethiopia — home to "Lucy," 10 minutes by car.

  • Holy Trinity Cathedral — historic landmark, 12 minutes by car.

  • Unity Park — peaceful retreat, 15 minutes by foot.

  • UN Economic Commission for Africa (UNECA) — 6 minutes on foot.

Practical Information

Arrival details

  • Valet and self-parking available on-site
  • Step-free accessible entrance from Ras Abebe Aregay Street
  • Complimentary airport shuttle, arranged in advance
  • 24-hour Front Desk for late or early arrivals
  • Secure luggage storage before check-in and after check-out
