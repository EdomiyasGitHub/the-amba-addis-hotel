Signature Restaurant

Buna & Vine

Modern Ethiopian plates, a 300-label wine list, and an open coffee-roasting counter — dinner only, in a room built around the ritual of Ethiopian coffee.

Buna & Vine takes its name from the Amharic word for coffee, buna, and treats it as a course rather than an afterthought: every dinner ends with a tableside roasting-and-brewing ceremony. The kitchen leans on regional produce from the Rift Valley and pairs each tasting course with a glass from a cellar of Ethiopian, South African, and European labels.
