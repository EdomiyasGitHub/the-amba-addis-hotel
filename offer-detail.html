Highland Honeymoon

A Highland Suite upgrade, a private terrace dinner, and a guided coffee ceremony for two — three nights built around slowing down.

Arrive to a Highland Suite dressed with fresh flowers and Ethiopian honey wine. On your second evening, our team sets a private table on your terrace for a five-course dinner prepared by the Buna & Vine kitchen. Your final morning includes a guided traditional coffee ceremony, hosted in the courtyard garden by our resident coffee steward.

What's included

  • 3 nights in a Highland Suite
  • Daily breakfast for two at Enat Kitchen
  • One private terrace dinner (5 courses, wine pairing included)
  • Guided traditional coffee ceremony for two
  • Late checkout, subject to availability
  • Bottle of Ethiopian honey wine on arrival
