Accessibility.

Last updated: January 1, 2026

  1. Home
  2. Accessibility

1. Our commitment

The Amba Addis is committed to making both this website and our physical property usable by as many people as possible, including guests with visual, auditory, motor, or cognitive disabilities. This website is designed and built to conform to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, Level AA — the standard we've held ourselves to throughout this project.

2. What we've done on this website

Specific measures built into this site include: semantic HTML landmarks and heading structure throughout; a "Skip to main content" link on every page; visible focus indicators on all interactive elements; sufficient color contrast between text and background across our Slate Blue & Copper palette; descriptive link text and image alternative text; forms with properly associated labels and error messaging; and interface components (navigation, accordions, carousels) that are operable by keyboard alone and that respect your device's "reduce motion" setting where animation is used.

3. Accessible rooms and facilities

The Highland Suite and select rooms in our east wing are step-free from the lobby via elevator, with widened doorways and roll-in showers with grab bars and a fold-down seat. Public areas, including our restaurant and the front desk, are step-free from the main entrance. If you have specific mobility, sensory, or other access needs, let us know when you enquire so we can confirm the right room and any additional arrangements — see our FAQ for related questions.

4. Known limitations

As with most accessibility work, this is an ongoing effort rather than a finished state. Some third-party embedded content on this site — such as the map in the footer — is provided by an outside service (OpenStreetMap) and its accessibility is outside our direct control. If you encounter a barrier anywhere on this site or during a stay, we want to know about it — see the contact details below.

5. Requesting accommodations

To request an accessible room, discuss a specific access need before booking, or report an accessibility barrier on this website, please contact us directly rather than through the demo booking form. We aim to respond to accessibility-related enquiries within one business day.
