Highland Suite

A separate living area, a deep soaking tub, and a private balcony over the Entoto hills — our most-requested suite for a second or third night in Addis.

The Highland Suite sits on the sixth and seventh floors, away from the street-facing rooms below. Furniture is hand-finished by a workshop in the Merkato district; the balcony faces west, so afternoon light fills the living area through the late hours. A stocked Nespresso station and a small library of Ethiopian design and travel writing are standard in every Highland Suite.

The Highland Suite is accessed by elevator; the bathroom has a step-in shower and soaking tub (not roll-in accessible). Guests needing a fully accessible room should choose the Garden Twin Room, located on the ground floor.
