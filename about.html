Our Story

Named for the highlands, built for how you actually travel.

"Amba" is the Amharic word for the flat-topped highland plateaus that define Ethiopia's landscape — a name that felt right for a hotel meant to sit a little apart from the noise of the city below it, without ever losing sight of it.

The Amba Addis sits above the city's rhythm — a boutique hotel where Ethiopian highland craft, third-wave coffee culture, and contemporary design meet. Rooms are quiet and considered. Service is warm without formality. We built this hotel for the guest who wants a real sense of place, not a copy of a hotel they've stayed in a dozen other cities.

Every material choice traces back to a workshop or a maker within a few hundred kilometers: hand-finished furniture from the Merkato district, woven textiles from the southern highlands, and a coffee program built around the ceremony itself, not just the cup.

Our Philosophy

Four ideas, held to consistently.

Not a mission statement for the wall — the actual standards every department is measured against.

A real sense of place

Design, food, and service that could only happen in Addis Ababa — never a generic template lifted from another city.

Craft over spectacle

Hand-finished furniture, a considered wine list, therapies built on regional botanicals — quality that rewards a second look.

Warm, not formal

Staff who remember your name and your coffee order by the second morning — hospitality that feels personal, not scripted.

Built to last, honestly

Sourced from regional workshops and suppliers wherever it's viable — practical stewardship rather than a marketing checkbox.

Our Team

Hospitality is a craft here, not a script.

Most of our department heads trained across East Africa and the Gulf before returning to Addis Ababa to open The Amba Addis. The result is a team that understands international service standards while never losing the specific, local warmth that makes a stay feel genuine rather than performed.

General Manager Selamawit Girma leads the property alongside Executive Chef Yonatan Bekele, whose kitchen anchors Buna & Vine, and Head of Guest Experience Marta Alemu, who trained in Dubai before returning home to Addis Ababa.
