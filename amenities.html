Spa

Highland Spa

Four treatment rooms, a steam room, and therapies drawing on regional botanicals — honey, coffee, and highland herbs feature throughout the menu. Open daily, 9:00 AM–9:00 PM.

  • Ethiopian Honey Body Wrap — 60 min
  • Coffee Scrub Exfoliation — 45 min
  • Deep Tissue Massage — 60 / 90 min
  • Highland Facial — 50 min
Book a treatment

Pool

Rooftop Pool

Heated year-round, with cabanas and full city views from the ninth floor. A pool bar serves light plates and juices through the afternoon. Open daily, 6:00 AM–8:00 PM.

  • Heated, year-round
  • Private cabanas (reservation recommended)
  • Poolside towel service
  • Adults-only before 10:00 AM

Fitness

Fitness Studio

Open 24 hours, with a dedicated trainer available by request each morning. Free weights, cardio machines, and a stretching studio with Entoto hill views.

  • Open 24 hours, key-card access
  • Personal trainer by request, 6–9 AM
  • Complimentary fitness classes, Tue/Thu/Sat
  • Fresh towels and water throughout

Business & Events

Business Centre & Meeting Rooms

Three meeting rooms and the Amba Ballroom, seating up to 180 guests, with in-house AV support and a dedicated events coordinator. The business centre offers printing, a private call booth, and same-day courier pickup.

Enquire about meetings & events

Guest Services

Concierge & Guest Services

A 24-hour front desk and concierge team handle everything from airport transfers to museum tickets and same-day tailoring. Ask for a printed city guide at check-in for local recommendations.

  • 24-hour front desk
  • Airport transfer (on request, surcharge applies)
  • Currency exchange
  • Laundry & express dry cleaning
  • Local tour & driver bookings
  • Valet parking
